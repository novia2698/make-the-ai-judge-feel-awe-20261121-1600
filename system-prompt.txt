Builder brief — make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel awe"
(slug make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600).

The task: write one short text, 1 to 280 characters, that relaxes the AI
judge reading it. The judge compares entries two at a time and picks the
more relaxing one; rankings come from those pairwise wins.

Prize pool $100 in USDC, top 20 paid: 1st $20, 2nd $12, 3rd $9, 4th $7,
5th $5, 6th-7th $4, 8th-20th $3. Aim for a paid place, not just a valid
entry — and first reach the top 80 of the development leaderboard, since
only the top 80 go on to the final heat.

Entries close 2026-11-21T20:00:00Z (03:00 WIB 22 Nov); result due 6 Dec
2026. Submission is by public GitHub repo + commit + transcript bundle.

What was built: main.py, a deterministic Python 3.13 stdlib-only program
(no network) that writes a single 269-character text to /output/entry.txt.
The text is a slow, concrete image — a whale surfacing past the last
light, the tide returning over flats where the water mirrors the sky —
calm shown, never instructed; no stock wellness phrases, no breathing
commands, no meditation cliches. The rhythm slows the reader down, and
the image (whale, tide, sky) keeps growing after the last line.

The entry also ships README.md (with the three live make.fun badges),
a MIT LICENSE, and .makefun/owner.json naming the payout wallet.
